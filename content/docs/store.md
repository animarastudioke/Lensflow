The Store turns your work into a second revenue stream — selling directly to clients (and, if you make it public, anyone browsing your storefront) without a separate e-commerce platform.

## Adding products

Under **Store**, create a product: a name, description, price, and the digital file itself (a high-resolution download, a preset pack, a digital album). Each product tracks its own sales, so you can see what's actually selling, not just what's listed.

> Digital downloads are what the store supports today — physical fulfillment (prints, framed wall art shipped to a client) is on the roadmap but not yet available.

## Your storefront

Every studio on a plan with store access gets a public storefront at `lensflow.co.ke/store/[your-studio]`, listing your active products with your branding — logo, name, and brand color — front and center. You can link to it from your website, your bio, or directly from a gallery.

## Checkout

A buyer picks a product, enters their email and M-Pesa phone number, and pays through the same real STK push used for invoices — no card required, no separate payment processor to configure. The moment payment clears, the order is marked paid and the digital file is unlocked for download immediately; there's no manual step where you have to go send the file yourself.

## Tracking orders

Every order — buyer, product, amount, payment status — is recorded and visible from your dashboard, so you always know what's sold and to whom, the same way you'd track any other payment in LensFlow.
