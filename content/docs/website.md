Most photographers end up paying for a website builder on top of their gallery and booking tools, then keeping all three in sync by hand. LensFlow's website builder works directly off the studio you've already set up, so there's nothing to duplicate.

## Building your site

Under **Website**, choose which of your galleries to feature, add your pricing and packages, and write the pages that make up your portfolio — home, about, contact. Your branding (logo and brand color) carries over automatically from Settings, so the site matches everything else a client sees.

## Connecting your own domain

By default your site is available on a LensFlow subdomain, but you can connect a custom domain you own — `yourstudioname.com` — so your portfolio, galleries, and booking flow all live under your own brand rather than a shared platform URL.

## Keeping it current

Because the site pulls from your actual galleries and pricing rather than a separate copy, publishing a new gallery or updating a package price updates your live site too — there's no second place to remember to edit.

## What it replaces

If you're currently paying for a standalone website builder (Squarespace, Wix, a photography-specific portfolio tool) alongside LensFlow, this is built to replace it — one login, one bill, and a site that's already wired to the galleries and booking flow your clients use.
